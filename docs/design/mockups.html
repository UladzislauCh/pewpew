Four steps, and not a single indicator. The bar under the header shows what is done, what is current and what is left.

Fix 3

A marker is edited where it lies

No separate editing block. Select, move, remove, add — all on the track, and all from the keyboard too.

03

Review

Analysis is over — and the person immediately hears the result instead of getting a toolbar. Shots are already replaced with a short default meme; they pick their own on the next step.

A marker cannot be checked by eye: on this track a second takes 32 pixels, and a 50 ms offset takes one and a half. But it is perfectly audible. So the screen asks a question out loud instead of asking you to read tick marks.

An error announces itself. The replacement is not layered on top — it mutes the original around the marker. Marker in place — the shot is not heard, the meme plays. Marker off — the mute covered silence, and the real bang stayed next to it: you get "bonk-bang". Anyone hears it, no need to read a waveform.

Listen to the whole clip, don't jump between markers. Stepping shows only the places where a marker exists — by construction it cannot show a missed shot, and every fourth or fifth shot is a miss. The clip is short, and it is their own highlight: watching it is not a cost.

The "next shot" button here is not a check but a skip: jump over twenty seconds of running between bursts.

The "no" answer is the only door to the tools. Until it is pressed, the track stays a map: you see how many shots there are and how they fell, but there is no reason to aim at it.

03 b

Edit

Opens only on the "no" answer. Here the track is a tool again — and here zoom finally makes sense: the person works with one spot, not the whole clip.

Going to a marker sets the zoom by itself. In the overview a burst of three shots takes six pixels — neighbouring markers three apart, hitting the right one is impossible. In a three-second window the same burst stretches to forty-seven, twenty-three between neighbours, and a double click stops being a lottery.

Stepping with "prev / next" buttons that play the surroundings — because precision must not depend on hitting two pixels with a mouse. Arrows move by one frame, a single step.

A double click distinguishes the target, not the action: hit a marker — removed; missed — placed.

A removed marker cannot be restored: the trash was postponed. A slip is fixed by another double click — cheap precisely because both actions live in the same place.

07

How it works

The copy is taken from shared/i18n/locales/ru.json, not invented. The four steps are numbered because it really is a sequence: the order carries meaning.

The limits are gathered in one line at the bottom — it is the same list as in the upload zone, and it must read the same in both places.

The footer gained a third column, "Соцсети" / "Social": YouTube and TikTok, icon plus name, opened in a new tab. It stands apart from "Обратная связь" — these lead off the site, those lead into the form. The columns are laid out with auto-fit, so on the 300 px screens (section 10) the third one simply drops to its own row; the focus ring on a social link is shown in section 08.

09

Feedback

A popup, not a page. The form opens where something went wrong and does not take you off the screen: the clip, the markers and the chosen sound stay as they were.

The topic is picked from a list, the rest of the form adapts to it. The popup knows where it was opened from: from marker editing it arrives with "Inaccurate markers" already set, from the 500 page with "Not working", from the footer with the topic the link named.

Submission goes to Web3Forms, not a Google form. The service's response is read, so "sent" is no longer taken on faith and the fallback link under the button is not needed. Didn't arrive — we say so, and what was written stays in the form.

Email is never required. Under the field it says honestly what that means: without an email we cannot reply.

Close it any way you like, the text is not lost. ×, Esc and a click outside close the popup, and what was written stays as a draft until sent: open it again — everything is there. This is cheaper than asking "really close?", and one mouse slip costs nothing.

From the keyboard: on open, focus goes to the first empty field, Tab moves only inside the popup, after closing focus returns to the link that opened it. The topic list — arrows and Enter. The focus ring is visible on every field.

The submit button is never grey. A missing sound link is an error under the field itself, at the moment of pressing, and focus moves to that field. A grey button keeps quiet about the reason; an error next to the field names it.

The roasted chicken already sits on both error pages — last round I wrongly dropped it for a big number. That way it is visible to whoever looks for the code to write to support, and it does not compete with the heading for attention.

The copy is yours, from errors in ru.json: "Round not found", "Empty here — like an eco round without a plant". No need to change it, it already speaks in the service's voice — I only added a second way out.

The difference between the two pages is in what to do. On 404 the address is at fault, and only going to the home page can help. On 500 we broke, so "Try again" comes first, with the report next to it.

COLOR

A palette for CS2

I would not change it wholesale. Your orange almost matches the amber accent of the game's own interface and the chicken in the logo — it is already "about CS2", just without saying so. The dark blue-grey background stays too: the game runs on dark, and a service next to it should not be blinding.

What is really missing is a third color. Right now everything that is not an action is grey, and a hint looks the same as a disabled button. I take the CT-side blue: it is from the same world but does not fight the orange — they are opposite on the wheel, and side by side each becomes more noticeable.

I leave the green as it is: it reads as "done" and does not go anywhere else.
